Turbo Modding RC Cars

Motivation

I bought some cheap rc-cars from the interwebs, out of curiosity. They work pretty well but the horsepower leaves a lot to be desired.

Then, I remembered that Tamiya sells replacement motors for their model cars, which happened to be compatible.

Building process

I started by replacing the motors. However, the original control board was unable to handle the increase current, and kept cutting out. This means that a new higher-power circuit is needed to truly unlock the potential of the motor.

I opted for a fully dedicated MOSFET H-bridge circuit. A bit of soldering and connections later and there is a clear improvement in horsepower, allowing the car to burnout its wheels and accelerate incredibly quickly.

To enable more fine-grained control of the throttle, I replaced the original control board with an ESP32-C3.

Software development process

I developed a simple control program for the ESP32-c3 using platformio, that receives controller input from an Android app. Using an Android app meant that I could use a large variety of joysticks to control the car.

The latency and unreliability of Bluetooth Low Power meant that many hacks were used in order to keep the controls as real-time as possible.
